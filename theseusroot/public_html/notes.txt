40. November 3, 2020 - A little background --this page depicting Theseus rushing into the coastal Greek town of Epidaurus is based upon my own experience entering the city of Chattanooga, Tennessee. Not because I've ever been chased by a club-wielding maniac while entering the city limits, but simply because I enjoy the experience in the exact same measure. Ironically, after a few minutes within a city that boasts its rich cultural credentials by installing non-objective expressionist sculptures between two decaying liquor stores, I silently beg for some unseen force to crush me with a murderous club and spare me yet another moment in the Scenic City where the scenery is comprised mostly of the impact of neglected building codes, concrete Soviet architecture and whimsically named restaurants.

42. November 10, 2020 - The classic Greek tale attributed to Aesop of the fox who lusts after the grapes --and is ultimately unable to reach them-- solidifies grapes' role as a food worthy of desire. Their obvious connection to wine and pleasure is shown by images of grape clusters adorning symposium pottery like the Exsekias-authored kylix known as the Dionysus Cup depicting the titular god reclined in a boat --the mast of which is entwined in grape-bearing vines. But never once in all of Hellenic culture do the Greeks care to acknowledge that grapes are just tiny, edible juice-balloons. And yet we credit them with understanding so much.
